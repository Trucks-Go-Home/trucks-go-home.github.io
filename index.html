Trucks Go Home

A calm, cozy puzzle game for Android.

Little clay trucks are parked all over the farm, and every one of them wants to go home. Tap a truck and it drives straight ahead, the way its nose is pointing. If another truck is in the way, it bumps and comes back. Find the truck that can leave first, then the next, until the whole farm is clear.

Sunny days, golden autumn, snowy winter and quiet nights under string lights, with new puzzles along the way: arrows, tunnels, hidden trucks, couples and a traffic light.
