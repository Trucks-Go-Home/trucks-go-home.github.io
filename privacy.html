Privacy Policy

Trucks Go Home · Last updated: 17 November 2026

Trucks Go Home is a puzzle game made by Ahmed Elwerdany (“we”). This policy explains what information the game uses and why. If you have a question, write to infoalwerdany+trucksgohome@gmail.com.

What the game keeps on your phone

The game saves your progress (the levels you've reached, your badges) and your settings (sounds, effects and other choices) on your phone. We don't run any servers and there are no accounts, so this information never reaches us.

If your phone backs up apps to your Google account, Android may include this save in that backup, so you can carry on after changing phones. That backup is handled by Google under Google's Privacy Policy; we can't see it.

Ads

Trucks Go Home shows ads from Google AdMob only when you choose to watch one, for example to get one more heart or more hints. Ads are limited to content suitable for general audiences.

To show and measure ads, Google may collect information from your phone, such as its advertising ID, IP address, approximate location, and how you interact with ads. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time from the game's Settings. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

How Google uses this information is explained at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Children

Trucks Go Home is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update it here and change the date at the top.
